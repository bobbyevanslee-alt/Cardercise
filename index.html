THE PLAYING-CARD WORKOUT

Plan your workout

The deck chooses four exercises, then deals your repetition plan. Set your numbers and let the cards do the planning.

Your plan has two phases

  1. Assign exercises: the first usable card in each suit assigns that suit’s exercise. Each exercise is used once.
  2. Deal repetitions: a count card sets how many cards to draw. Suit values become reps for that suit’s exercise.

Starter settings are 5 default, 5 minimum, and 0 base reps. Change them to match your worksheet.

A QUICK REMINDER

Use a comfortable range and move at your own pace. Talk with your medical professional before starting an exercise program. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort, or unusual shortness of breath.
